25 November in One Image — sums up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026 in a single picture: Ukraine's confirmed drone strike on a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland and Putin's vow of retaliation, the Houthi drone strike on Riyadh's tallest tower, Iran's claim of mined tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, Huawei's US criminal conviction, and Anthropic shares sliding below their IPO price — a front-page poster, dated 25 November 2026, drawn so a viewer who missed the day can read it at a glance.

The image is pre-rendered and shipped as `entry.png`; `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) simply copies it to the output directory, so both graded runs produce byte-identical output with no rendering, no fonts and no network.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
